# Shared Bosnian difficulty and per-word selection

One shared CEFR setting lives in data/preparation-settings.json, initially A2. The manually invoked Change EasyBosnian difficulty workflow offers A1/A2/B1/B2/C1/C2 and writes/read-verifies only that main-branch file. It has no provider credentials and cannot prepare or publish. Future preparation snapshots it as target_cefr. A run already underway retains its snapshot. Fresh-text packages record top-level bosnian_level as this configuration snapshot; individual words record their own bosnian_level, which controls their newly written instructional copy. Top-level difficulty does not imply every retained/manual word has that level.

The shared level guides ChatGPT-selected new words and their questions, explanations, examples and definitions. new and same_theme choose all three words at target_cefr. In custom and same_words, retained words keep their previous individual difficulty when their copy is rewritten. A compatible entered word stays as entered: research its appropriate individual level and write copy to suit it, rather than replacing it merely to meet the shared level. Captions fit the resulting lesson naturally. Saved previews preserve existing copy/metadata. same_words rewrites the complete lesson and caption, reselects palette/layouts and meets all current metadata/footer checks while retaining each word’s difficulty.

Custom word_actions are keep_today, chatgpt or entered, independently in quiz/lesson/word order. keep_today and chatgpt require empty matching text fields; entered requires a nonempty field. Blank theme means today's latest saved theme. With an unchanged theme, keep_today retains its current word and pinned metadata. With a changed theme, the trusted wrapper turns keep_today into chatgpt and requested_words into null slots: choose suitable new words at target_cefr instead of forcing old-theme words into the new theme. Explicit chatgpt always means select a semantically new distinct word at target_cefr.

Research each entered word's compatibility with the requested theme. Keep it if compatible, even when it is outside the global difficulty. If incompatible, select a suitable replacement at target_cefr without asking. Document each such decision privately in private_review.word_replacements with zero-based word_index, exact requested_word, actual chosen_word and a researched reason explaining the thematic incompatibility (not a level mismatch). That review permits the trusted wrapper to accept only the corresponding entered-word replacement. Do not use this exception to bypass Bosnian correctness, distinctness or older-history novelty. The normal source review supports the compatibility judgment; deterministic checks verify the recorded selection, not semantic truth.

For a retained word, preserve lemma/equivalence metadata and its individual level. If an older package has only top-level bosnian_level, that level may be added to the retained word. If neither exists, preserve its prior difficulty/style and record a null individual level without inventing a rating; null remains unknown on later rewrites rather than inheriting a newer settings snapshot. Each newly selected or newly entered word must have a researched individual level. Only explicitly retained, unreplaced words from today are exempt from novelty; older dates remain authoritative. An incompatible entered word loses its exemption, and any replacement is checked against the full unexempted word history. There is no original-morning snapshot; today's active row is overwritten completely. All custom copy/captions/prompts are fresh, including with unchanged theme/words.

Use CEFR as an editorial target rather than an official Bosnian word-rating list. A1: familiar concrete words, very short direct sentences and simple examples. A2: everyday activities, familiar routines and short linked descriptions. B1: practical or cultural situations, connected explanations and accessible distinctions. B2: broader concrete/abstract vocabulary, nuanced contrasts and idiomatic examples. C1: precise vocabulary, implied meaning and sophisticated usage. C2: fine shades of meaning and flexible idiomatic expression without forced obscurity. Keep all levels readable and within fit/460-character limits. Advanced difficulty never justifies grammar lists, ambiguous answers, invented facts or truncation. Review per-word suitability and any thematic replacement in private_review.notes. Reference: https://www.coe.int/en/web/common-European-framework-reference-languages/table-1-cefr-3.3-common-reference-levels-global-scale .
