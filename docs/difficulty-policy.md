# Shared Bosnian difficulty and per-word selection

One shared CEFR difficulty is stored in data/preparation-settings.json, initially A2. The manually invoked Change EasyBosnian difficulty workflow offers A1/A2/B1/B2/C1/C2 and updates only that main-branch JSON file with the repository's contents token; it prepares/publishes nothing and has no provider credentials. Future preparation snapshots that configuration into target_cefr in build/preparation-context.json. Fresh-text content records bosnian_level equal to target_cefr. The level guides newly selected vocabulary and all rewritten questions, definitions, explanations, examples and captions; manually supplied/pinned words remain exactly selected. same_words and saved previews preserve all existing text and metadata, even when the global level has changed. Do not add a level field or website footer to an image-only preserved package.

Custom word_actions are keep_today, chatgpt or entered, independently in quiz/lesson/word order. keep_today uses today's latest saved word and requires an empty matching text field. chatgpt requires an empty field and a null requested_words slot: choose a semantically new distinct Bosnian word at target_cefr, compared with the full unexempted history. entered requires the matching nonempty field and preserves that requested word. Blank custom theme means today's latest saved theme. There is no original-morning snapshot; same-date history stays a complete overwrite. Existing words explicitly retained via keep_today/entered can reuse today's metadata, but older rows are never exempted. All custom copy/captions/prompts are freshly generated at the global level, including when every action keeps today's words.

Use CEFR as an editorial difficulty target rather than an official Bosnian word-rating list. A1: familiar concrete words, very short direct sentences and simple examples. A2: common everyday activities, familiar routines and short linked descriptions. B1: practical or cultural situations, connected explanations and accessible distinctions. B2: broader concrete/abstract vocabulary, nuanced contrasts and idiomatic examples. C1: precise vocabulary, implied meaning and natural sophisticated usage. C2: fine shades of meaning and flexible idiomatic expression without forced obscurity. Keep every level concise, readable and within the unchanged fit/460-character limits; advanced difficulty does not justify grammar lists, ambiguous answers, invented facts or truncation. Research all language claims and briefly explain level suitability in private_review.notes for fresh-text content. Reference: https://www.coe.int/en/web/common-European-framework-reference-languages/table-1-cefr-3.3-common-reference-levels-global-scale .
