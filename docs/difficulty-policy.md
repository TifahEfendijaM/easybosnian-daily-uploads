# Shared Bosnian difficulty and per-word selection

Difficulty is now selected once per preparation, never read from data/preparation-settings.json. The publishing workflow difficulty menu offers random/A1/A2/B1/B2/C1/C2 for new, same_theme and newly chosen custom words. Default random uses exact probabilities A1 50%, A2 25%, B1 12.5%, B2 6.25%, C1 3.125%, C2 3.125% (C2 receives the remaining tail). The 05:00 Worker keeps its existing dispatch and therefore uses random/new by default. The trusted wrapper freezes selection.target_cefr/context.target_cefr before research and the staging validator uses that same snapshot, without another draw or reading legacy settings. Retained/manual words keep their individual ratings; same_words/same_images keep the source batch's target (random only if missing) and ignore the dropdown. A compatible entered word is rated individually. Saved previews keep exact old content and difficulty. The old configure-level.yml and settings code/file are deliberately retained, but its job is disabled and those settings no longer affect publishing. No schedule, cleanup, retry, credential or published-only history change.

The frozen run target guides ChatGPT-selected new words and their questions, explanations, examples and definitions. new and same_theme choose all three words at target_cefr. In custom and same_words, retained words keep their previous individual difficulty when their copy is rewritten. A compatible entered word stays as entered: research its appropriate individual level and write copy to suit it, rather than replacing it merely to meet the shared level. Captions fit the resulting lesson naturally. Saved previews preserve existing copy/metadata. same_words rewrites the complete lesson and caption, reselects palette/layouts and meets all current metadata/footer checks while retaining each word’s difficulty.

Custom word_actions are keep_today, same_image, chatgpt or entered, independently in quiz/lesson/word order. keep_today, same_image and chatgpt require empty matching text fields; entered uses its matching field, with an empty/unusable word delegated to ChatGPT. Blank theme means the selected batch’s theme. With an unchanged theme, keep_today retains its current word and pinned metadata. With a changed theme, the trusted wrapper turns keep_today into chatgpt and requested_words into null slots: choose suitable new words at target_cefr instead of forcing old-theme words into the new theme. Explicit chatgpt always means select a semantically new distinct word at target_cefr.

Research every entered or kept custom seed before writing its lesson. First try to identify the intended Bosnian word when it has an unambiguous spelling error: for example, Cijenkati se → cjenkati se. If the researched correction fits the theme and passes Bosnian validity, theme-fit and within-batch distinctness checks (historical repeats are allowed for this manual choice), preserve that intended word instead of selecting an unrelated alternative. Record cause=spelling_correction, rebuild its valid equivalence metadata, and assess the corrected word's own individual difficulty as for a compatible entered word. A mere mismatch with the run target is not an error.

If the intended word cannot be established or validated, or an entered/kept seed fails thematic compatibility, standard Bosnian validity, within-batch distinctness or usability, select a suitable new seed for only that slot at target_cefr. Keep other valid slots. For Znaš li?, the replacement may remain a comparison/topic appropriate to the lesson. Document each correction/fallback in private_review.word_replacements: zero-based word_index, exact requested_word, actual chosen_word, a researched reason of 20–400 characters, and cause from spelling_correction/theme_mismatch/invalid_bosnian/word_overlap/unusable_seed. Match the actual entered or keep_today slot; already delegated chatgpt slots need no replacement review. Never use history_repeat as a fallback cause: manual/kept seeds may repeat history. The schema still reads historical saved reviews with that cause, but new preparation rejects it. Historical entered-word reviews without cause retain their theme_mismatch meaning; new reviews must include cause, and kept-word reviews require it.

The wrapper delegates structurally unusable seeds before research: empty entered fields, absent keep_today sources, over-180-character or non-word-only inputs, and repeated exact normalized seeds (first occurrence remains). The selection context records these indices/causes in word_input_fallbacks and supplies null requested_words/chatgpt actions for them. Nonempty text paired with keep_today/chatgpt/same_image, invalid action values or an unusable theme are configuration errors, not word substitutions.

Correct/fallback within the same unreviewed draft and research execution, before ready; do not automatically rerun the workflow, Codex execution or any publication. Rebuild affected card/word metadata and copy plus caption/quiz review, then run check_selection, schema/editorial/history/secrecy validation and measured feed/Story fit again. Other schema, theme, research-access, credentials, caption, image-prompt or fit failures still block until genuinely repaired. The private review permits selection changes, not a waiver of those checks. Deterministic checks validate the recorded selection; source research/reviewer checks establish its linguistic and thematic truth.

For a retained word, preserve lemma/equivalence metadata and its individual level. If an older package has only top-level bosnian_level, that level may be added to the retained word. If neither exists, preserve its prior difficulty/style and record a null individual level without inventing a rating; null remains unknown on later rewrites rather than inheriting a newer run snapshot. Each newly selected or newly entered word must have a researched individual level. Explicit custom themes and entered/kept word equivalents may repeat any prior batch, even when they are absent from the latest source. A researched spelling correction remains the intended manual choice and may also repeat. Only null/delegated ChatGPT slots and genuinely different fallback choices must be semantically new against every batch. Apply novelty_history(history,date,selection,package) to the final checked package so manual aliases are exempt while generated choices remain checked. Never reject, replace or report semantic_novelty solely because an explicit manual choice repeats history. All within-batch distinctness, research, language, theme-fit, answer-secrecy and measured-fit checks remain mandatory. Mark semantic_history_checked only after this selection-aware review, not by claiming manual repeats are new. There is no original-morning snapshot; only the selected latest batch is overwritten; none preserves it and adds a new batch. All custom copy/captions/prompts are fresh, including with unchanged theme/words.

Use CEFR as an editorial target rather than an official Bosnian word-rating list. A1: familiar concrete words, very short direct sentences and simple examples. A2: everyday activities, familiar routines and short linked descriptions. B1: practical or cultural situations, connected explanations and accessible distinctions. B2: broader concrete/abstract vocabulary, nuanced contrasts and idiomatic examples. C1: precise vocabulary, implied meaning and sophisticated usage. C2: fine shades of meaning and flexible idiomatic expression without forced obscurity. Keep all levels readable and within fit/460-character limits. Advanced difficulty never justifies grammar lists, ambiguous answers, invented facts or truncation. Review per-word suitability and any thematic replacement in private_review.notes. Reference: https://www.coe.int/en/web/common-European-framework-reference-languages/table-1-cefr-3.3-common-reference-levels-global-scale .

For the Znaš li? slot, “word” also means its lesson seed: a comparison or short Bosnian topic is allowed. Assess the difficulty of the complete lesson and its taught terms, not just the seed string. Entered comparisons/topics receive their own reviewed level; retained seed metadata/level stays exact. The public heading may describe the comparison or topic independently of the internal seed.

Every newly prepared caption weaves the actual lesson difficulty into a natural sentence in its selected caption language, connected to the theme rather than a standalone label. Use caption_levels(package): mention the shared actual level or only the lowest/highest endpoints of a mixed lesson; do not relabel retained words with a new run target. English captions may say “Practise everyday conversations in Bosnian at level A2”; Bosnian captions may say “Danas vježbamo bosanski na nivou B2”. These are examples, not fixed slogans. If any retained rating is unknown, preserve null and address the configured target audience naturally without claiming a rating. Set caption_language using the language policy, review accuracy and answer secrecy, preserve caption limits and end with 👉easybosnian.com. Fresh preparation/save requires require_caption_level=True; saved previews are not rewritten.

The full same_images mode still retains all three photos/words and ignores custom fields. The trusted context lists reused_photo_kinds in attachment order; inspect exactly those supplied photos, keep their corresponding previous prompts unchanged, and write fresh content appropriate to them. Omit BOTH background_source and background_sources from model drafts: the wrapper assigns provenance after verifying unchanged source bytes. Ordinary invalid entered/kept words retain their existing reviewed fallback policy. latest/none/all_today identity, cleanup scope, published-only history and no-retry rules remain unchanged. A retained original may stay privately cached when its old history row is pruned but another batch explicitly references it; retain its original 14-day expiry, never extend it. Deploy the backward-compatible Worker source reader before merging the client/menu; no D1 migration or extra live publication test is needed.


## Explanation language

Language policy for fresh content: lesson/word definitions and explanations (card.body) and quiz questions are in natural English when that individual word/lesson is rated A1 or A2; B1–C2 explanations/questions stay standard Bosnian. Set each non-quiz card.body_language and the quiz card.question_language to en/bs according to its own assigned word level. Set private_review.language_policy_checked=true only after actually reviewing English accuracy, the language of every question/explanation and the Bosnian portions. Quiz answer choices always remain Bosnian, including Da/Ne; words, headings, examples, hashtags and alt text also remain Bosnian. Caption language follows the actual lesson levels through caption_language(package): use English when there is no known B1–C2 content and the highest level returned by caption_levels(package) is A1 or A2, otherwise standard Bosnian. A mixed A1/A2 beginner batch is English; any known B1–C2 content makes its caption Bosnian. If a retained legacy rating is unknown, use the existing target-audience fallback from caption_levels without inventing word ratings. Set package.caption_language=en/bs and review the actual prose before setting private_review.language_policy_checked=true. Regenerated same_words/same_images captions follow retained actual levels, not a different new dropdown value. Saved legacy previews remain unchanged. An A1/A2 quiz may ask an English description such as “Which word means the male parent?” with Bosnian options; never put the correct Bosnian answer or an option letter in the question. Keep the same exact reviewed question/options in feed, Story and native-poll caption handling; no renderer translation. Unknown legacy individual levels stay Bosnian rather than inventing a rating. Preserve exact Bosnian examples and diacritics. Do not replace the Bosnian featured word/title or option strings with English translations; an English body may naturally define their meaning, and never leak the quiz answer or identifying hints through an English explanation. Do not ask for translation of a foreign word in the quiz. Use descriptions, situations or visual naming in the question language required by the quiz word’s individual level. Internal FLUX prompts and research/equivalence metadata may remain English. Existing saved previews are read unchanged; only newly researched copy follows this policy.
