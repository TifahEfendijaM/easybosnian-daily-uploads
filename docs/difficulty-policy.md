# Shared Bosnian difficulty and per-word selection

One shared CEFR setting lives in data/preparation-settings.json, initially A2. The manually invoked Change EasyBosnian difficulty workflow defaults to Keep current, which reads the actual main-branch setting without writing or resetting it. Select A1/A2/B1/B2/C1/C2 to change it; the run summary confirms the actual saved level after read-back. GitHub’s native dropdown uses a static YAML default and does not load this JSON dynamically. The configuration action writes/read-verifies only that main-branch file. It has no provider credentials and cannot prepare or publish. Future preparation snapshots it as target_cefr. A run already underway retains its snapshot. Fresh-text packages record top-level bosnian_level as this configuration snapshot; individual words record their own bosnian_level, which controls their newly written instructional copy. Top-level difficulty does not imply every retained/manual word has that level.

The shared level guides ChatGPT-selected new words and their questions, explanations, examples and definitions. new and same_theme choose all three words at target_cefr. In custom and same_words, retained words keep their previous individual difficulty when their copy is rewritten. A compatible entered word stays as entered: research its appropriate individual level and write copy to suit it, rather than replacing it merely to meet the shared level. Captions fit the resulting lesson naturally. Saved previews preserve existing copy/metadata. same_words rewrites the complete lesson and caption, reselects palette/layouts and meets all current metadata/footer checks while retaining each word’s difficulty.

Custom word_actions are keep_today, same_image, chatgpt or entered, independently in quiz/lesson/word order. keep_today, same_image and chatgpt require empty matching text fields; entered uses its matching field, with an empty/unusable word delegated to ChatGPT. Blank theme means the selected batch’s theme. With an unchanged theme, keep_today retains its current word and pinned metadata. With a changed theme, the trusted wrapper turns keep_today into chatgpt and requested_words into null slots: choose suitable new words at target_cefr instead of forcing old-theme words into the new theme. Explicit chatgpt always means select a semantically new distinct word at target_cefr.

Research every entered or kept custom seed before writing its lesson. First try to identify the intended Bosnian word when it has an unambiguous spelling error: for example, Cijenkati se → cjenkati se. If the researched correction fits the theme and passes Bosnian validity, theme-fit and within-batch distinctness checks (historical repeats are allowed for this manual choice), preserve that intended word instead of selecting an unrelated alternative. Record cause=spelling_correction, rebuild its valid equivalence metadata, and assess the corrected word's own individual difficulty as for a compatible entered word. A mere mismatch with the global difficulty is not an error.

If the intended word cannot be established or validated, or an entered/kept seed fails thematic compatibility, standard Bosnian validity, within-batch distinctness or usability, select a suitable new seed for only that slot at target_cefr. Keep other valid slots. For Znaš li?, the replacement may remain a comparison/topic appropriate to the lesson. Document each correction/fallback in private_review.word_replacements: zero-based word_index, exact requested_word, actual chosen_word, a researched reason of 20–400 characters, and cause from spelling_correction/theme_mismatch/invalid_bosnian/word_overlap/unusable_seed. Match the actual entered or keep_today slot; already delegated chatgpt slots need no replacement review. Never use history_repeat as a fallback cause: manual/kept seeds may repeat history. The schema still reads historical saved reviews with that cause, but new preparation rejects it. Historical entered-word reviews without cause retain their theme_mismatch meaning; new reviews must include cause, and kept-word reviews require it.

The wrapper delegates structurally unusable seeds before research: empty entered fields, absent keep_today sources, over-180-character or non-word-only inputs, and repeated exact normalized seeds (first occurrence remains). The selection context records these indices/causes in word_input_fallbacks and supplies null requested_words/chatgpt actions for them. Nonempty text paired with keep_today/chatgpt/same_image, invalid action values or an unusable theme are configuration errors, not word substitutions.

Correct/fallback within the same unreviewed draft and research execution, before ready; do not automatically rerun the workflow, Codex execution or any publication. Rebuild affected card/word metadata and copy plus caption/quiz review, then run check_selection, schema/editorial/history/secrecy validation and measured feed/Story fit again. Other schema, theme, research-access, credentials, caption, image-prompt or fit failures still block until genuinely repaired. The private review permits selection changes, not a waiver of those checks. Deterministic checks validate the recorded selection; source research/reviewer checks establish its linguistic and thematic truth.

For a retained word, preserve lemma/equivalence metadata and its individual level. If an older package has only top-level bosnian_level, that level may be added to the retained word. If neither exists, preserve its prior difficulty/style and record a null individual level without inventing a rating; null remains unknown on later rewrites rather than inheriting a newer settings snapshot. Each newly selected or newly entered word must have a researched individual level. Explicit custom themes and entered/kept word equivalents may repeat any prior batch, even when they are absent from the latest source. A researched spelling correction remains the intended manual choice and may also repeat. Only null/delegated ChatGPT slots and genuinely different fallback choices must be semantically new against every batch. Apply novelty_history(history,date,selection,package) to the final checked package so manual aliases are exempt while generated choices remain checked. Never reject, replace or report semantic_novelty solely because an explicit manual choice repeats history. All within-batch distinctness, research, language, theme-fit, answer-secrecy and measured-fit checks remain mandatory. Mark semantic_history_checked only after this selection-aware review, not by claiming manual repeats are new. There is no original-morning snapshot; only the selected latest batch is overwritten; none preserves it and adds a new batch. All custom copy/captions/prompts are fresh, including with unchanged theme/words.

Use CEFR as an editorial target rather than an official Bosnian word-rating list. A1: familiar concrete words, very short direct sentences and simple examples. A2: everyday activities, familiar routines and short linked descriptions. B1: practical or cultural situations, connected explanations and accessible distinctions. B2: broader concrete/abstract vocabulary, nuanced contrasts and idiomatic examples. C1: precise vocabulary, implied meaning and sophisticated usage. C2: fine shades of meaning and flexible idiomatic expression without forced obscurity. Keep all levels readable and within fit/460-character limits. Advanced difficulty never justifies grammar lists, ambiguous answers, invented facts or truncation. Review per-word suitability and any thematic replacement in private_review.notes. Reference: https://www.coe.int/en/web/common-European-framework-reference-languages/table-1-cefr-3.3-common-reference-levels-global-scale .

For the Znaš li? slot, “word” also means its lesson seed: a comparison or short Bosnian topic is allowed. Assess the difficulty of the complete lesson and its taught terms, not just the seed string. Entered comparisons/topics receive their own reviewed level; retained seed metadata/level stays exact. The public heading may describe the comparison or topic independently of the internal seed.

Every newly prepared caption weaves the actual lesson difficulty into a natural Bosnian sentence connected to the theme, instead of a standalone label. Use caption_levels(package): when all three individual word/lesson levels are known, mention their shared level or only the lowest/highest endpoints for a mixed lesson (for example “Kroz svakodnevne situacije danas vježbamo bosanski na nivou B2.” or “Današnji primjeri povezuju gradivo od nivoa A1 do B2.”). Do not relabel retained lower-level copy with a newer global setting. If any retained legacy level is unknown, preserve that null rating and address the configured target audience naturally instead (for example “Učite bosanski na nivou B2? ...”), without claiming an official rating for those words. Vary the sentence to fit this batch; never copy these examples mechanically. Review the wording and level/range privately, keep all prose Bosnian, preserve quiz-answer secrecy and the caption limit, and leave 👉easybosnian.com on the final line. Fresh preparation/save requires require_caption_level=True; existing saved previews are not rewritten merely to add a level.

## Individual retained photographs

Custom word actions now also include same_image, independently for Pitanje dana, Znaš li? and Riječ dana. It keeps that role’s latest selected word/lesson seed, pinned metadata, exact original photograph and background_prompt, while regenerating instructional text, quiz where relevant, examples, captions and the photo-assessed design. Leave its typed word field empty. Other slots keep their selected keep_today/chatgpt/entered behavior and get new photos. The full same_images mode still retains all three photos/words and ignores custom fields. The trusted context lists reused_photo_kinds in attachment order; inspect exactly those supplied photos, keep their corresponding previous prompts unchanged, and write fresh content appropriate to them. Omit BOTH background_source and background_sources from model drafts: the wrapper assigns provenance after verifying unchanged source bytes. Individual source overrides use background_sources[kind]={batch_id,artwork_id}; background_source remains the common source for other cards and older rows. These fields are private cache provenance, not publication state. Later mixed/full photo reuse resolves each role independently. Only unselected roles call FLUX. A missing saved source, an expired/ambiguous original or an incompatible frozen word/photo stops before generation and cleanup; do not silently replace a same_image word/photo, even after a theme change. Ordinary invalid entered/kept words retain their existing reviewed fallback policy. latest/none/all_today identity, cleanup scope, published-only history and no-retry rules remain unchanged. A retained original may stay privately cached when its old history row is pruned but another batch explicitly references it; retain its original 14-day expiry, never extend it. Deploy the backward-compatible Worker source reader before merging the client/menu; no D1 migration or extra live publication test is needed.
