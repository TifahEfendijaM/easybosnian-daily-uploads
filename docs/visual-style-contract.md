# Trusted visual rendering contract

ChatGPT selects approved declarative data AFTER inspecting actual generated photos. Pillow validates and renders exact reviewed text. `designs.py`, `data/design-palettes.json` and the strict schema in `visual_placement.py` define live enums; `hybrid.py` owns drawing/measurement. Recovery and exact-card retention follow `element-design-recovery.md`. Preparation/FLUX scene rules belong to `preparation-task.md`, not visual review.

## 1. Inputs and composition decisions

The reviewer sees ONLY complete actual photographs and exact feed crops, exact public copy, compact measured fit profiles and whitelisted recent accepted-choice metadata (up to five completed daily runs, including reruns). No rendered/styled references or private answer keys. `default_plan` is solely an uncertainty fallback, never a design recommendation. Missing recent context is explicitly incomplete, not invented, and does not block fresh review.

Review ALL compatible catalogue options before choosing; catalogue order/familiarity is not a ranking. Compare several materially different treatments and plausible upper/middle/lower, left/right or split placements against each photo's negative space, exact text length, hierarchy, subject visibility and overall balance. A top placement is valid when best; it has no automatic preference. Neither rare styles nor variety itself are goals. If recent accepted treatments/visually similar families have dominated, prefer a different suitable, appealing, readable, harmonious alternative, even if slightly less optimal alone; never an unsuitable choice. This design-history preference does not override compatibility, safety or full-photo rules.

Fresh lesson/word cards and non-picture-identification quizzes MUST use full-background hybrid with photo_window=null. Separate inset/middle photo windows are reserved ONLY for visual_identification quizzes, where the task is to identify/name what is depicted; those keep the complete contain photo and answers underneath. Long lesson/word text is not an exception: use compatible bounded panel styles, independent placements, widths and typography over the full photo. Editorial/cover pages and plain body/italic_footer remain archive-readable, not fresh feed choices. Explicit same_image&design retention stays byte-for-byte; Story treatment B is unchanged. The strict model schema and production validation apply this restriction to initial proposals, repairs and every local recovery candidate; the legacy renderer can still reproduce archived compositions.

Choose per-card palettes independently against natural materials, light, dominant colours and readable panel/text contrast. Give all 36 approved palettes equal consideration, including light/dark and new/familiar options; catalogue order is not a ranking. Neither light nor dark palettes or panel tones have a default preference. Assess palette harmony and panel tone independently and compare materially different suitable light/dark alternatives for the actual photo. Base the brightness-history preference on actual bounded-panel surfaces recorded by panel_theme/panel_theme_observations, never palette names or catalogue groupings. Apply it symmetrically: after a clear streak of light panels, gently favour a suitable darker treatment; after a clear streak of dark panels, gently favour a suitable lighter treatment. This is ONLY a tiebreaker when alternatives fit similarly well. A clearly better fit for the photograph, readability and palette harmony wins over this brightness preference even if it continues either streak. Palette selection stays independent; no palette gets priority merely for sounding light or dark. Mixed/unknown observations are not evidence of either streak; do not invent missing history. No permanent brightness bias, fixed light/dark ratio, brightness quota, forced alternation or random selection. No shared-batch/quiz-matching or blue/yellow brand-palette preference; never recolour photos. The preparation palette is fallback only. Compare EVERY approved palette, without first/last/familiar shortcuts, then apply the following existing repetition exception:

- For EVERY palette, normally at most TWO uses in any consecutive NINE image cards including the candidate. Count the preceding eight known applied palettes and earlier choices in this batch in quiz→lesson→word order.
- Also avoid a SECOND use in the SAME BATCH. At either threshold compare all other approved palettes and select a suitable alternative even if the repeated one is slightly preferable. Only when absolutely no other palette suits the photo may `palette_exception=no_other_suitable_palette` permit reuse; otherwise `none`.
- Never choose clashing colours or use random rotation. This alternatives-first rule takes precedence over a repeated single best match, not over suitability. Observed fallback palettes count for later choices but are not forcibly recoloured. Non-palette recent-choice context excludes fallback decisions and earlier fixed/random example presets; palette provenance remains independent.

## 2. Declarative fields and typography

Cards expose `confidence` (high/uncertain), `design` (hybrid/editorial), approved `palette`, `palette_exception`, `category_style`, `category_tone`, `heading_tone`, `heading_fade`, `heading_shade`, `photo_window`, and ordered `blocks`. Blocks expose role/style, x/y/width, font_size, line_spacing, optional max_lines (null or 1–12) and wrapping (balanced/natural). Body/answers also expose panel_tone and panel_opacity=100; answers alone expose marker_style, examples alone photo_tone/fade_style/shade_strength. Preserve every exact source role in array order; array order need not impose vertical order except ordered quiz choices.

No proposal text/manual line breaks, drawing code/primitives, CSS, font filenames, custom colours/hex, arbitrary opacity, border/radius/shadow/filter definitions or custom rendering instructions. Category/title anchors and fonts/sizes remain renderer-controlled. `design_template` is legacy-readable, ignored by rendering and omitted from fresh content.

| Role | Target size | Loaded face | Leading multiplier |
|---|---|---|---|
| body | 32–48px | Inter Regular | 1.20–1.55 |
| answers | 32–46px | Inter Regular | 1.20–1.55 |
| example | 28–40px | Lora Italic | 1.20–1.55 |

Pillow computes final height from exact glyphs and actual baselines, with at least 4px between adjacent accented rows; a single line has no inter-line collision. Balanced wrapping distributes the minimum lines without tiny orphans; natural wrapping is greedy. Unicode spaces reconstruct exact source; NBSP/narrow NBSP/word joiners remain unbreakable. Never strip/normalize diacritics or combining characters for drawing. max_lines is a rejection constraint, never truncation permission. Category letters use actual glyph bounds and a shared capital baseline so Š/Č accents do not shift the lettering downward.

Use the original transparent logo small and unobtrusive, normally bottom-right with safe clearance and its alpha preserved. No white square, large logo banner or blue “EasyBosnian [date]” footer. Keep 1080×1350 feed and 1080×1920 Story exports with consistent safe margins.

Fit profiles include exact text/width/font/leading, complete height, line count, padding, label and separator. Measurement-family deduplication never merges style renderers. Profiles are data, not a restricted creative menu: all approved intermediate widths/fonts/leading/natural wrapping remain available. Do not scale heights linearly across widths/fonts or estimate an example from a body profile with different wrapping. A leading-only change adjusts height by (line_count−1) × change in rounded font_size*line_spacing, subject to glyph clearance.

## 4. Categories, panel colours and opacity

Categories: rounded (moderate corners), pill (capsule), ribbon (pointed right end), underlined (bare text/rule), outlined (paper chip/accent outline), tab (square accent tab/inset rule). All keep fixed Inter typography/anchors and correctly aligned Bosnian capital accents. Category elements NEVER add their own fade/scrim/glow/shadow, including underlined labels. Bare labels must pass glyph contrast against the final photo without adding a category-owned shade; choose a bounded chip otherwise. Pillow rejects unreadable bare labels rather than adding shade or silently changing their design. The smooth title fade may extend behind the category area. Category elements are composited after all photographic shades, clearly on top; bare-label contrast is still checked against the final shaded photo.

Panel/category tones use approved palette roles, named text_then_surface: auto (style default), ink_paper, paper_ink, ink_accent, accent_ink. For EVERY bounded body/answer style compare light surfaces/dark text and dark palette-ink surfaces/bright paper or accent text EQUALLY; paper_ink/accent_ink selects the latter independently of the card shape. Catalogue paper/ink descriptions are auto defaults, not a preference or restriction. Do not use auto by habit. Choose against the actual photo, palette, visual weight and readability; neither light nor dark is preferred and there are no frequency quotas or random alternation. Plain editorial body requires auto/100. Every fresh bounded body/answer panel is fully opaque at 100%, including repairs/fallback/reuse: no random draw or model-selected transparency. Glyphs, badges/rules remain opaque. Category chips, unboxed copy and preset fades are separate controls.

## 5. Title and separate PRIMJER treatments

Title heading_tone allows auto, paper_dark, ink_light, paper_ink, ink_paper, accent_dark, accent_light. Title text may use neutral white/black or palette paper/ink/accent; its backing is ONLY neutral BLACK or WHITE. Actively compare ALL compatible approved title text colours, including palette paper/ink/accent, rather than habitually settling on white/black text. PRIMJER photo_tone independently keeps ALL neutral and palette-coloured pairings in PHOTO_TONE_CATALOGUE. Give neutral black/white and EVERY approved palette-coloured PRIMJER shade backing equal consideration: do not automatically favour neutral backings or treat palette-coloured backings as secondary exceptions. Compare every compatible pairing and choose the approved text/backing combination best suited to the actual photograph, palette harmony and readability. Light text on dark remains the usual direction; choose dark text/light backing when the photograph suits it better, not solely for variety. This brightness/contrast preference does not favour black over approved dark palette colours or white over approved light palette colours. Equal consideration does not mean random selection, equal usage frequency or forced variety.

For lesson/word titles ONLY (Znaš li? and Riječ dana), assess readability WITHOUT shading first. If the intended colour is unreadable or barely readable, FIRST consider changing the text to harmonious approved palette ink, paper or accent, then compare the other compatible approved text colours on the actual photograph. Prefer a comfortably readable palette-matching unshaded title (for example, dark palette ink on a clear light wall). ONLY consider shading when the lesson/word title would otherwise remain unreadable or barely readable after considering these colour changes. Do not retain white/cream plus a shade by habit when a palette colour works clearly on the bare photo. No decorative/balance/emphasis exception for these two card types. Do not force clashing or poorly readable text solely to avoid necessary protection. Quizzes are EXEMPT: photographic quiz titles may still have shading purely for visual emphasis when it suits the photograph.

Fresh title heading_fade is ONLY standard, with smoothly eased edges for both black and white backings. Sharp/crisp/defined title borders are unavailable. Trusted equal 24px upper/lower shoulders extend the smooth fade on both sides of the title, followed by equal 100px tapers. The upper fade may reach behind the category area; the category element is composited clearly on top. The full smooth taper may reach body panels or PRIMJER backings without being clipped at their edges; panels/text render above photographic fades and final pixel contrast remains mandatory. Whenever used, the title backing MUST span the ENTIRE image width edge-to-edge, with equal opacity along each horizontal row for black and white alike; no text-width patch or lateral dimming. Keep its faintest contrast-safe strength and smooth vertical tapers. This sets the edge treatment only IF shading is needed; it does not require a shade. PRIMJER keeps its separate standard/sharp choices. Necessary lesson/word title shading stays gentle where readability allows. Light/gentle means subtle strength/transparency, NOT a light backing colour: when black backing/light approved text and white backing/dark text BOTH suit the photograph sufficiently well, STRONGLY prefer black backing with light text, even if white is slightly preferable alone. White remains available when clearly better for readability/harmony or black cannot meet contrast/caps. Use an explicit compatible heading_tone rather than auto by habit; approved coloured title text stays available. This preference applies only when title shading is appropriate and never overrides the lesson/word unshaded-first rule or shade caps. Photographic quizzes have no PRIMJER, so strongly consider a harmonious title backing without applying the dual-shade concern. ChatGPT chooses heading_shade=gentle or stronger per photographic quiz: gentle keeps weakest-sufficient shading (including none when readable); stronger deliberately adds a more opaque approved backing when suited to the photograph, even when bare text already passes contrast. Neither is mandatory. Lesson/word and paper/treatment-B headings require gentle; paper headings do not gain photographic shading. Older saved plans without heading_shade retain gentle behavior.

PRIMJER has ONLY italic_photo (unshaded photo, must pass as-is), italic_photo_fade (photo with trusted contrast backing), italic_footer (unboxed editorial paper footer/separator). All use Lora Italic and renderer-owned PRIMJER label, never a box. For italic_photo_fade choose standard (smooth compact taper) or sharp (smoothly blended TOP edge, crisp straight BOTTOM edge, no lower taper/rounding/drawn border), equally considered with neither preferred; compare the actual photo rather than alternate/randomize. Both extend 18px BELOW the final row; standard has a 48px taper, sharp keeps 12px core padding above the label where protected space permits, with a compact 48px upper taper and NO lower taper. Use inactive standard for unshaded/photo-footer and paper headings; paper heading/footer tones use auto. No fade is added to italic_photo.

Pillow alone owns fade geometry and actual opacity: ChatGPT may select only the approved PRIMJER strength ID, never numeric opacity or custom dimensions/softness. Titles retain 100px extent; standard uses power 2.5, defined retains distinct linear edges. Title alpha candidates are 12,24,36,48,54,66,78,90,102,114,126,130,144,170,210,240, with fine 2-alpha steps for white where available. Use the first passing 3:1 glyph contrast, except the photographic quiz stronger preset starts at trusted alpha 90 (about 35% under the letters) and escalates only for measured contrast. This qualitative enum never exposes numeric opacity, geometry or an arbitrary strength to ChatGPT; category layering and the gentle outer profile stay intact. The chosen title alpha applies across the full width at each row, without horizontal falloff; the existing vertical easing and equal upper/lower shoulders remain. PRIMJER shade_strength exposes exactly soft, medium and strong. Give all three equal consideration per photograph/backing/text/visual balance, with no weak/strong preference, random selection or equal frequency rule. The trusted alpha targets are 64,128,192 (about 25%,50%,75%); the approved ladder is 64,90,128,130,170,192,210,240. Pillow keeps the selected minimum and escalates only for 4.5:1 actual glyph contrast. An explicit photographic-fade choice renders the requested backing even when bare text is readable. Missing strength in older plans retains the original contrast-driven 90,130,170,210,240 ladder. Unshaded italic_photo and paper italic_footer use inactive soft and never gain a shade. Strength is independent of edge preset and neutral/palette-coloured pairing; no custom numeric opacity is accepted. Reports show the requested strength and measured applied opacity when available. This does not change title behaviour. Text remains above the logo strip; its shade may extend into the footer before the unchanged original logo is composited on top.

Judge the combined weight of title AND example shades across the WHOLE photo, even far apart. For lesson/word titles, the readability-first rule takes precedence: keep a title backing only when still necessary after considering readable palette text colours. ONLY when their combined weight actually looks heavy/striped/crowded, strongly prefer shading only ONE; when the title is readable bare, prioritise the PRIMJER backing rather than the title backing. If both need shading, favour a readable composition with lighter title shading and stronger support for PRIMJER, rather than the reverse, whenever readability allows. The larger title can usually stand on its own; the smaller italic example benefits more from a backing. Achieve this hierarchy through approved text colours and independent example placement; Pillow alone controls actual strength, with no custom strength/opacity field. Unshaded italic_photo and both shades remain available when appropriate. Do not add unnecessary shade merely to enforce the preference, sacrifice contrast/harmony or suppress required title protection. Readability takes priority; this is not a global change to either shade preset. The relative PRIMJER-over-title priority does not favour any absolute PRIMJER shade strength.

## 6. Independent placement and geometry

Body and photographic PRIMJER are independent, not an attached pair. The example may be far BELOW an upper body near the logo-safe boundary, far ABOVE a lower body or elsewhere when best. There is no maximum gap, required adjacency or imposed vertical order; hundreds of pixels can be intentional. When the explanation sits high near the title, STRONGLY PREFER PRIMJER lower in the photograph near the logo-safe boundary, leaving a substantial open gap instead of clustering all text at the top. Compare safe lower placements first; close-under-body is appropriate only when those lower alternatives obscure an important subject/action, cannot remain harmonious/readable with suitable approved text/backing/strength combinations, or cannot fit safely. Choose its text/backing colours, standard/sharp edges and soft/medium/strong strength against the actual lower photo region, not by inheriting the upper treatment or defaulting to strong shading. A low body may still pair with an example far above. Close grouping remains available subject to this upper-body preference. Maintain 32px minimum example clearance for EVERY shade/design, not a target distance. Editorial/footer and Story order stays fixed.

Prefer one exact-copy example line whenever it fits safely at the chosen font: do not create artificial wrapping with a narrow column. Pillow may widen ONLY the example and shift it horizontally to the nearest tested safe slot, preserving y/font/leading/text/style/colours/other blocks, then recheck everything. If unsafe/unreadable retain original wrapping; no truncation/font shrinking/other-block movement or extra model call for this adjustment. Report applied x/width changes. Long examples wrap normally.

Blocks stay within x64..1016, below header_end+24 (fixed feed heading starts y174), ending by y1170 above the logo. Editorial italic_footer alone may end by y1286 with right edge≤x880. Body/answers need ≥8px measured separation (14–24px breathing room preferred); editorial body/photo/footer need 24px. Panels occupy their FULL rectangles; unboxed collision bounds use visible glyph/label/rule width, not unused wrapping space. Declared geometry still stays safe.

Photo windows fix x64/width952, y250..770, height400..800 (240..800 for visual identification). hybrid uses contain; editorial cover. A contained photo is complete, with photographic examples at least 20px inside its actual bounds. Body/answers stay outside separate windows; visual-naming quizzes require question → complete contained photo → all choices. Panel coverage cannot exceed 49% of the card. Semantic face/object/action preservation remains the visual model's judgment; geometry cannot guarantee arbitrary subject detection.

## 7. Validation, recovery, provenance and compatibility

Pillow loads bundled licensed Inter body/categories, Lora SemiBold titles and Lora Italic examples, measures wrapping/final heights and rejects unsupported fields/combinations, reordered/lost roles, invalid sizes/leading, clipping/truncation, header/logo/window collisions, overlap, excessive coverage and unreadable pixels. Body/example glyph contrast is ≥4.5:1, titles ≥3:1, sampled at actual glyph masks against final composited surfaces, including neighbouring fades. Valid proposals are never moved for aesthetics.

Layout and palette acceptance are independent. Missing/unapproved/low-contrast palette may be replaced with the preparation palette while preserving a valid model layout; a valid model palette may survive failed elements/layout. For photo contrast failure try the unchanged layout with the preparation palette before discarding safe layout choices. Uncertain confidence replaces layout but can keep a valid palette; unavailable/malformed outer review uses full fallback. Isolatable per-card/element failures do not discard valid siblings. Apply the bounded position repair, up-to-three proposals and smallest-element recovery in element-design-recovery.md, preserving accepted fields and rechecking collisions/pixels. Integrity failures remain fatal. Full fallback's PRIMJER targets the lower safe edge (bottom y1150), moving upward only as measured height requires; an unreadable/unrenderable final fallback stops publication.

Initial visual review deadline is 600 seconds, defect-feedback proposals 180; service failures receive no retry. Store fixed safe rejection reasons and only schema-approved rejected parameters BEFORE fallback, even if fallback fails; unknown prose becomes unclassified. Reports separately show layout (chatgpt/mixed/reused/fallback), palette source/reason, preserved/replaced elements, requested/applied position/width adjustments, design attempts and photo inspection/selected attempt. Historical missing provenance stays unknown, never inferred from matching IDs; compatibility outcome is layout-only. Do not report successful repair as full fallback or expose arbitrary model prose, private notes, answers or credentials.

Stories retain trusted treatment B, exact source photo/copy and final per-card palette/category choice, with Inter body and unboxed Lora Italic footer plus accent separator; no independent design call. Prepared JPEGs are versioned and reused unchanged after credential persistence. Legacy design_template, soft/defined example fades, soft/defined title fades and earlier opacity strings/numeric values/omitted defaults remain readable, never fresh choices. Earlier fixed/random/opacity report provenance stays historically accurate, not rewritten or treated as current model choices. Keep current manifest/version handling; no Worker/database/scheduler change is implied by this contract.

Non-quiz title strength is capped at 126/255, matching the recorded 25 October Hoćemo li unutra? reference. When PRIMJER starts less than 100px below the title text boundary, the title cap is 78/255: the first preference is NO title shade, comparing all harmonious approved title text colours first. Only if an unshaded title cannot remain comfortably readable may it receive faint protection under this lower cap. An unreadable capped title is a design rejection for colour/placement repair, never permission to publish unreadable text or exceed the limit. Quiz title emphasis and PRIMJER strengths remain independent.
