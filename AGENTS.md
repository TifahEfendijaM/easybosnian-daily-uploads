Quiz imagery must meaningfully relate to the question and the day's theme. Decide what the reader is being asked to discover before writing the image prompt. In a visual naming quiz, showing the object is the question itself: ask clearly in Bosnian what is pictured and show one unmistakable referent. If the question already gives a subject, the photograph may depict that subject. When the quiz tests an unknown fact or a hidden identity, use relevant context that does not solve that unknown. Never substitute an unrelated room, wall or landscape just to avoid a clue. Do not show the correct option letter, written answer, ticks or visual comparison of the choices. Keep answer text/aliases/explanation out of captions, hashtags, alt text and the other cards. For visual quizzes, alt text describes the task without naming the target. Privately review why the scene is relevant and what, if anything, it gives away.

Avoid routine conjugation/declension lists, isolated spelling drills and grammar trivia. Do more than repeat a dictionary definition.
